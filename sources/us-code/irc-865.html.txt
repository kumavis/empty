#  26 U.S. Code § 865 - Source rules for personal property sales 

  * U.S. Code 
  * Notes 
  * Authorities (CFR) 


[prev](/uscode/text/26/864 " Definitions and special rules") | next 

(a) General ruleExcept as otherwise provided in this section, income from the [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) of personal property—

(1)

by a [United States resident](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-826863439-397779289&term_occur=999&term_src=) shall be sourced in the United States, or

(2)

by a [nonresident](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-2032936163-397779289&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) shall be sourced outside the United States.

(b) Exception for inventory propertyIn the case of income derived from the [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) of [inventory property](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1044147225-397777367&term_occur=999&term_src=)—

(1)

this section shall not apply, and

(2)

such income shall be sourced under the rules of sections 861(a)(6), 862(a)(6), and 863.

Notwithstanding the preceding sentence, any income from the [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) of any[ unprocessed timber ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-2088764766-1193708693&term_occur=999&term_src=)which is a softwood and was cut from an area in the United States shall be sourced in the United States and the rules of sections 862(a)(6) and 863(b) shall not apply to any such income. For purposes of the preceding sentence, the term [“unprocessed timber”](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-2088764766-1193708693&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) means any log, cant, or similar form of timber.

(c) Exception for depreciable personal property

(1) In generalGain (not in excess of the [depreciation adjustments](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-10829459-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)) from the[ sale ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=)of [depreciable personal property](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1824942377-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) shall be allocated between sources in the United States and sources outside the United States—

(A)

by treating the same proportion of such gain as sourced in the United States as the [United States depreciation adjustments](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-713302004-397783131&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) with respect to such property bear to the total[ depreciation adjustments,](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-10829459-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) and

(B)

by treating the remaining portion of such gain as sourced outside the United States.

(2) Gain in excess of depreciation

Gain (in excess of the [depreciation adjustments](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-10829459-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)) from the[ sale ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=)of [depreciable personal property](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1824942377-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) shall be sourced as if such property were[ inventory property.](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1044147225-397777367&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)

(3) United States depreciation adjustmentsFor purposes of this subsection—

(A) In general

The term “[United States depreciation adjustments](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-713302004-397783131&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)” means the portion of the[ depreciation adjustments ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-10829459-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)to the adjusted basis of the property which are attributable to the[ depreciation deductions ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-2067070241-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)allowable in computing taxable income from sources in the United States.

(B) Special rule for certain propertyExcept in the case of property of a kind described in section 168(g)(4), if, for any taxable year—

(i)

such property is used predominantly in the United States, or

(ii)

such property is used predominantly outside the United States,

all of the [depreciation deductions](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-2067070241-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) allowable for such year shall be treated as having been allocated to income from sources in the United States (or, where clause (ii) applies, from sources outside the United States).

(4) Other definitionsFor purposes of this subsection—

(A) Depreciable personal property

The term “[depreciable personal property](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1824942377-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)” means any personal property if the adjusted basis of such property includes[ depreciation adjustments.](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-10829459-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)

(B) Depreciation adjustments

The term “[depreciation adjustments](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-10829459-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)” means adjustments reflected in the adjusted basis of any property on account of[ depreciation deductions ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-2067070241-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)(whether allowed with respect to such property or other property and whether allowed to the taxpayer or to any other person).

(C) Depreciation deductions

The term “[depreciation deductions](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-2067070241-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)” means any deductions for depreciation or amortization or any other deduction allowable under any provision of this chapter which treats an otherwise capital expenditure as a deductible expense.

(d) Exception for intangibles

(1) In generalIn the case of any [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) of an[ intangible—](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-966942115-397782171&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)

(A)

this section shall apply only to the extent the payments in consideration of such [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) are not contingent on the productivity, use, or[ disposition ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-583380919-317025006&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)of the[ intangible,](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-966942115-397782171&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) and

(B)

to the extent such payments are so contingent, the source of such payments shall be determined under this part in the same manner as if such payments were royalties.

(2) Intangible

For purposes of paragraph (1), the term “[intangible](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-966942115-397782171&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)” means any patent, copyright, secret process or formula, goodwill, trademark, trade brand, franchise, or other like property.

(3) Special rule in the case of goodwill

To the extent this section applies to the [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) of goodwill, payments in consideration of such [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) shall be treated as from sources in the country in which such goodwill was generated.

(4) Coordination with subsection (c)

(A) Gain not in excess of depreciation adjustments sourced under subsection (c)

Notwithstanding paragraph (1), any gain from the [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) of an[ intangible ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-966942115-397782171&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)shall be sourced under subsection (c) to the extent such gain does not exceed the [depreciation adjustments](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-10829459-397783130&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) with respect to such[ intangible.](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-966942115-397782171&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)

(B) Subsection (c)(2) not to apply to intangibles

Paragraph (2) of subsection (c) shall not apply to any gain from the [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) of an[ intangible.](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-966942115-397782171&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)

(e) Special rules for sales through offices or fixed places of business

(1) Sales by residents

(A) In general

In the case of income not sourced under subsection (b), (c), (d)(1)(B) or (3), or (f), if a [United States resident](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-826863439-397779289&term_occur=999&term_src=) maintains an office or other fixed place of business in a foreign country, income from[ sales ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=)of personal property attributable to such office or other fixed place of business shall be sourced outside the United States.

(B) Tax must be imposed

Subparagraph (A) shall not apply unless an income tax equal to at least 10 percent of the income from the [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) is actually paid to a foreign country with respect to such income.

(2) Sales by nonresidents

(A) In general

Notwithstanding any other provisions of this part, if a [nonresident](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-2032936163-397779289&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) maintains an office or other fixed place of business in the United States, income from any[ sale ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=)of personal property (including [inventory property](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1044147225-397777367&term_occur=999&term_src=)) attributable to such office or other fixed place of business shall be sourced in the United States. The preceding sentence shall not apply for purposes of [section 971](/uscode/text/26/971) (defining export trade corporation).

(B) Exception

Subparagraph (A) shall not apply to any [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) of [inventory property](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1044147225-397777367&term_occur=999&term_src=) which is sold for use,[ disposition,](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-583380919-317025006&term_occur=999&term_src=) or consumption outside the United States if an office or other fixed place of business of the taxpayer in a foreign country materially participated in the[ sale.](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)

(3) Sales attributable to an office or other fixed place of business

The principles of [section 864(c)(5)](/uscode/text/26/864#c_5) shall apply in determining whether a taxpayer has an office or other fixed place of business and whether a[ sale ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=)is attributable to such an office or other fixed place of business.

(f) Stock of affiliatesIf—

(1)

a [United States resident](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-826863439-397779289&term_occur=999&term_src=) sells stock in an[ affiliate ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1588692301-397777364&term_occur=999&term_src=)which is a foreign corporation,

(2)

such [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) occurs in a foreign country in which such [affiliate](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1588692301-397777364&term_occur=999&term_src=) is engaged in the active conduct of a trade or business, and

(3)

more than 50 percent of the gross income of such [affiliate](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1588692301-397777364&term_occur=999&term_src=) for the 3-year period ending with the close of such [affiliate](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1588692301-397777364&term_occur=999&term_src=)’s taxable year immediately preceding the year in which the[ sale ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=)occurred was derived from the active conduct of a trade or business in such foreign country,

any gain from such [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) shall be sourced outside the United States. For purposes of paragraphs (2) and (3), the [United States resident](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-826863439-397779289&term_occur=999&term_src=) may elect to treat an[ affiliate ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1588692301-397777364&term_occur=999&term_src=)and all other corporations which are wholly owned (directly or indirectly) by the[ affiliate ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1588692301-397777364&term_occur=999&term_src=)as one corporation.

(g) United States resident; nonresidentFor purposes of this section—

(1) In generalExcept as otherwise provided in this subsection—

(A) United States residentThe term “[United States resident](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-826863439-397779289&term_occur=999&term_src=)” means—

(i) any individual who—

(I)

is a United States citizen or a resident alien and does not have a tax home (as defined in [section 911(d)(3)](/uscode/text/26/911#d_3)) in a foreign country, or

(II)

is a [nonresident](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-2032936163-397779289&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) alien and has a tax home (as so defined) in the United States, and

(ii)

any corporation, trust, or estate which is a United States person (as defined in [section 7701(a)(30)](/uscode/text/26/7701#a_30)).

(B) Nonresident

The term “[nonresident](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-2032936163-397779289&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)” means any person other than a [United States resident](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-826863439-397779289&term_occur=999&term_src=).

(2) Special rules for United States citizens and resident aliens

For purposes of this section, a United States citizen or resident alien shall not be treated as a [nonresident](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-2032936163-397779289&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) with respect to any[ sale ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=)of personal property unless an income tax equal to at least 10 percent of the gain derived from such[ sale ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=)is actually paid to a foreign country with respect to that gain.

(3) Special rule for certain stock sales by residents of Puerto [Rico](/topn/racketeer_influenced_and_corrupt_organizations_act_rico)Paragraph (2) shall not apply to the [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) by an individual who was a bona fide resident of Puerto [Rico](/topn/racketeer_influenced_and_corrupt_organizations_act_rico) during the entire taxable year of stock in a corporation if—

(A)

such corporation is engaged in the active conduct of a trade or business in Puerto [Rico](/topn/racketeer_influenced_and_corrupt_organizations_act_rico), and

(B)

more than 50 percent of its gross income for the 3-year period ending with the close of such corporation’s taxable year immediately preceding the year in which such [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) occurred was derived from the active conduct of a trade or business in Puerto [Rico](/topn/racketeer_influenced_and_corrupt_organizations_act_rico).

For purposes of the preceding sentence, the taxpayer may elect to treat a corporation and all other corporations which are wholly owned (directly or indirectly) by such corporation as one corporation.

(h) Treatment of gains from sale of certain stock or intangibles and from certain liquidations

(1) In generalIn the case of gain to which this subsection applies—

(A)

such gain shall be sourced outside the United States, but

(B)

subsections (a), (b), and (c) of section 904 and sections 907 and 960 shall be applied separately with respect to such gain.

(2) Gain to which subsection appliesThis subsection shall apply to—

(A) Gain from sale of certain stock or intangiblesAny gain—

(i)

which is from the [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) of stock in a foreign corporation or an[ intangible ](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-966942115-397782171&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)(as defined in subsection (d)(2)) and which would otherwise be sourced in the United States under this section,

(ii)

which, under a treaty obligation of the United States (applied without regard to this section), would be sourced outside the United States, and

(iii)

with respect to which the taxpayer chooses the benefits of this subsection.

(B) Gain from liquidation in possessionAny gain which is derived from the receipt of any distribution in liquidation of a corporation—

(i)

which is organized in a possession of the United States, and

(ii)

more than 50 percent of the gross income of which during the 3-taxable year period ending with the close of the taxable year immediately preceding the taxable year in which the distribution is received is from the active conduct of a trade or business in such possession.

(i) Other definitionsFor purposes of this section—

(1) Inventory property

The term “[inventory property](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1044147225-397777367&term_occur=999&term_src=)” means personal property described in paragraph (1) of section 1221(a).

(2) Sale includes exchange

The term “[sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=)” includes an exchange or any other[ disposition.](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-583380919-317025006&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865)

(3) Treatment of possessions

Any possession of the United States shall be treated as a foreign country.

(4) Affiliate

The term “[affiliate](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-1588692301-397777364&term_occur=999&term_src=)” means a member of the same affiliated group (within the meaning of [section 1504(a)](/uscode/text/26/1504#a) without regard to section 1504(b)).

(5) Treatment of partnerships

In the case of a partnership, except as provided in regulations, this section shall be applied at the partner level.

(j) RegulationsThe Secretary shall prescribe such regulations as may be necessary or appropriate to carry out the purpose of this section, including regulations—

(1)

relating to the treatment of losses from [sales](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=title:26:subtitle:A:chapter:1:subchapter:N:part:I:section:865) of personal property,

(2)

applying the rules of this section to income derived from trading in futures contracts, forward contracts, options contracts, and other instruments, and

(3)

providing that, subject to such conditions (which may include provisions comparable to [section 877](/uscode/text/26/877)) as may be provided in such regulations, subsections (e)(1)(B) and (g)(2) shall not apply for purposes of sections 931 and 933.

(k) Cross references

(1)

For provisions relating to the characterization as dividends for source purposes of gains from the [sale](/definitions/uscode.php?width=840&height=800&iframe=true&def_id=26-USC-3522631-397777366&term_occur=999&term_src=) of stock in certain foreign corporations, see section 1248.

(2)

For sourcing of income from certain foreign currency transactions, see section 988.
